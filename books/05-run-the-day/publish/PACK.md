## Description

A data center day is not managed because the rooms looked calm. This field guide is for the manager on duty. It says what you own, who may touch the plant, which service you actually sold, which work is permitted, and how a change differs from maintenance.

The reader leaves with one day sheet: handover, permits, the round, the vendor, the incident clock, and the capacity question you are not allowed to invent. A blank line is a part of the day you have not managed.

This book is not a facilities-operations course and not a summary of one. Standards are titles. Client plans stay in the site file.
