# 21. The shape of the opinion

Readers of a financing file will not read your notebook. They will read the first pages and then search for their own question. Put the opinion where they will look, and make it narrower than the appendices.

Open with the scope and the line you did not cross, before any description of the hall. A description that comes first becomes the finding. People quote descriptions. They skip limits that arrive on page forty.

Then the sentence you will sign, and the sentence you will not, in the same section. Then the findings that change a relied-on sentence, five lines each. Then the look: documents by revision, hours on the floor, rooms not entered, sample rule and what you actually sampled. A reader who disagrees with you should be able to see the look without calling you.

Appendices hold the questionnaires, the photograph index if any, the lists you are allowed to attach. They do not hold a second opinion in softer words. If the appendix says the team is strong and the opinion says the stop could not be said, the appendix is a false document. Cut it.

Do not organize the report by how impressed you were, room by room, tour order. Organize it by the contract sentences. Power for the performance they sell. Identity of the equipment they bought. The chair who owes the night. A tour order hides a missing sentence in a room you enjoyed.

Number findings. A lender must be able to say finding 3 is open. A prose paragraph with three problems will be remembered as one mood. Moods get negotiated away. Numbers do not, unless a document closes that number.

A reader who only has the executive pages must still see the limits. Repeat the line you did not cross, and the sentence you will not sign, on those pages. Do not leave them for the body. Bodies are where limits go to be ignored. If the executive pages cannot hold the no, they are too long, or the no is being saved for later. Later is how a yes gets forwarded alone.

## Decisions to record

- Opinion before description.
- Limits in the same section as the yes.
- Findings numbered. Appendix does not contradict them.
- Order follows the contract sentences, not the tour.
