# 13. When the window is short

Some deliveries give you days, not a season. The method does not become a tour because the window is short. It becomes a narrower sentence.

Do the four walk acts, smaller, and say so. A full count if the list exists. An identity sample you can finish, under a rule you wrote that morning only if the engagement allows a rule that late. Prefer the rule from the night before. A running-state sample, a few machines, not a feeling about the row. Witness of the customer's test, or acceptance of their pass document, with the verb accurate. If you cannot stay for the test, you have the document or you have nothing. Do not reconstruct the test from a conversation.

Drop, explicitly, what the window cannot support. You did not rerun commissioning. You did not audit the operating system. You did not measure power quality. You did not establish a failure rate. The report that pretends those happened is the false document. A short true report is the work.

Prioritize by the contract sentence. If the financing depends on the equipment being the equipment they bought, identity and count come before a tour of the cooling plant. If it depends on the room staying up, the power path comes before the market slide. A short window spent on the interesting room is a failed visit even if the notes are long.

Write the hours you had, and the rooms you did not enter. Unentered is not reviewed. A host who fills the hours with presentations has used the window. Ask for the floor. If you do not get it, the opinion says the plant was not seen.

## Meetings are not floor time

A kickoff, a management presentation, and a lunch in which the architecture is explained do not count as hours on the floor. Write them separately. If the day is six hours and five of them are slides, you had one hour. The opinion may not sound like a day on site. It sounds like one hour, plus the documents you had already read. That is more useful to a lender than a warm account of a hosted visit.

Ask at the start of the day for the floor time in the calendar, not at the end when the car is waiting. If they will not put it in, you still go to the floor and you write that the schedule did not include it. A schedule that did not include the floor is a finding about the visit, when the engagement was to see the plant.

## Triage Prioritization Protocol for Compressed 24-Hour Site Walk Windows

In institutional M&A acquisitions and project finance underwriting for mission-critical data centers, every qualitative observation recorded during the site walk must be translated into a deterministic commercial or contractual parameter for the investment committee and lending syndicate:

$$\text{Priority}_{\text{walk}} = \arg\max_k \left( \text{Probability}_{\text{SPOF}, k} \times \text{CapEx}_{\text{remedy}, k} \right)$$

An observation that merely remarks on plant appearance without quantifying its impact on **Firm Deliverable IT Capacity ($\text{MW}$)**, **Single-Point-of-Failure ($\text{SPOF}$) Availability**, **Immediate Catch-Up Capital Expenditure ($\text{CapEx}$)**, or **Conditions Precedent ($\text{CP}$) / Conditions Subsequent ($\text{CS}$)** fails to protect the relying party.

## Decisions to record

- Hours on the floor, separate from hours in a meeting.
- Rooms not entered.
- Acts finished: count, identity, running sample, witness. Each yes or narrowed.
- Claims the window cannot support, listed in the opinion.
