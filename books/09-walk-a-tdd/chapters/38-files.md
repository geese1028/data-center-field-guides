# 38. Two files, not one notebook

You will accumulate more than the opinion can hold. Split it the day you start, or their identifiers will leak into the thing you send.

The working file holds the asset lists, the serials, the photographs, the interview names, the drawings, the contract copies. It stays where the engagement says it stays. It is not a template for the next visit. It is not a folder you sync to a place the next project can see. When the engagement says to delete or return it, you do. A personal archive of client lists is not a methodology.

The opinion file holds the sheet, the numbered findings, the revisions you read, and the sentences. It should still make sense if every photograph and every serial were removed. If a sentence dies without the picture, rewrite the sentence before you send. Chapter 29. Names of people belong in the working file unless the opinion needs a role. "The person on the roster for that hour" is the role. Their name is not the finding.

Version the opinion. The flag list is a version. The draft they comment on is a version. The sent opinion is a version. Do not edit the sent version into a smoother story when a late document arrives. A late document is a new note: finding number, closed or not, date. Silent edits are how a lender's copy and your copy diverge, and you will not remember which one you signed.

Backups of the working file are their risk and yours. A second person on your side must be able to open what the engagement requires you to keep, without the only copy living on a laptop that travels. That is the same backup rule as a verification pack. A laptop is not a data room. If you cannot name the second person, you do not yet have a file. You have a bag.

Do not paste this book's sentences into their report and file the result as both. Their report is their voice, evidenced. This book is the method. Mixing them is how a template finding appears in a hall you did not visit.

## Two-File Information Security Architecture: Site Working Notes vs. Sanitized Opinion

In institutional M&A acquisitions and project finance underwriting for mission-critical data centers, every qualitative observation recorded during the site walk must be translated into a deterministic commercial or contractual parameter for the investment committee and lending syndicate:

$$\text{File}_{\text{client\_deliverable}} \cap \text{File}_{\text{raw\_site\_identifiers}} = \text{Controlled Mapping Only}$$

An observation that merely remarks on plant appearance without quantifying its impact on **Firm Deliverable IT Capacity ($\text{MW}$)**, **Single-Point-of-Failure ($\text{SPOF}$) Availability**, **Immediate Catch-Up Capital Expenditure ($\text{CapEx}$)**, or **Conditions Precedent ($\text{CP}$) / Conditions Subsequent ($\text{CS}$)** fails to protect the relying party.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Audit Objective** | Two-File Information Security Architecture: Site Working Notes vs. Sanitized Opinion | Cross-check VDR contracts against physical nameplates & test logs | Convert unverified claims into explicit CP/CS or valuation haircuts |
| **Sampling & Evidence Rule** | Curated tour of flagship hall or summary slide | Statistical random sample ($n = \lceil\sqrt{N}\rceil + 1$) chosen by auditor | Any sample mismatch triggers expanded $100\%$ population audit |
| **Redundancy & Capacity Gate** | Nameplate sum of generators, chillers, or transformers | Derate for $99.6\%$ ASHRAE extreme wet-bulb and N+1/2N fault paths | Lock underwritten revenue model to firm derated MW ceiling |
| **Financial Protection Mechanism** | Seller warranty representation in purchase agreement | Quantify remediation cost $C_{\text{fix}}$ + schedule delay risk | Require $150\%\text{--}200\%$ cash escrow holdback at financial close |

## Decisions to record

- Working file and opinion file split before the first note.
- Opinion still true with photographs and serials removed.
- Sent version not silently edited. Late closes are new notes.
- Second person who can open the working file, or the file is not yet kept.
- Template sentences not pasted into their report.
