# 11. What you do not calculate

A due diligence accumulates numbers because lenders like numbers. Most of them are not yours.

You do not compute a failure rate for equipment that has no rate in a document you may cite, and then multiply it into a probability that three will fail together. That product looks like engineering. It is a story. If the manufacturer has not published a figure you are allowed to use, you do not borrow one from "industry practice." Industry practice is the word the handover book already refused. The practical control is the spare, the procedure, and the roster. Write those. Leave the probability out.

You do not convert a nameplate, a design kilowatt, or a brochure runtime into the operating case. You do not add them. You do not fill a capacity cell to seem complete.

You do not score the team. "Technically strong" is a mood. The evidence is whether they could say the stop, whether the sample matched the record, and whether the open items were on the page. Moods do not survive a question from counsel.

You do not invent a sample percentage in this book for every visit. The visit writes its own rule before arrival and follows it. A rule copied from a previous report, including one you wrote, is another hall. State this visit's rule.

You do not put a market growth rate, a share, or a competitiveness adjective in the technical opinion. If the engagement forces a market section, it is a separate section, labeled not a finding, and you do not let the opinion cite it.

## A theory of failure is still a theory

You will be tempted by a short calculation. Take a figure you half remember for how often a class of machine fails. Multiply by the count. Multiply again for the chance that several fail at once. The product is small, so the service level looks safe. The product is not evidence. You do not have their failure figure. A borrowed figure is not a test. A small product will be quoted as your engineering judgment that simultaneous failure can be ignored. That is the sentence this chapter exists to stop.

The control that belongs in the opinion is ordinary and checkable. Is there a spare for the failure the contract assumes. Is there a person. Is there a paper that stops at a safe state. Did you see them, or only hear them. Those three sentences do the work the probability was meant to do, without inventing a rate. If any of the three is missing, the service level is not evidenced. You do not need a tiny number to say that. A tiny number would hide it.

## Decisions to record

- The probability you refused to print.
- The capacity cell you left blank.
- The sample rule of this visit, not of the last one.
- Market text kept out of the opinion, or absent.
