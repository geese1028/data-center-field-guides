# 39. A buyer and a lender do not get the same extra

The sheet is the same. The temptation is not. A lender asks whether the assumption in the financing is evidenced, and what would break it. A buyer asks whether they will inherit a plant they can live with. Both questions can be answered from the same look. They cannot be answered by adding the sentences the other one wanted.

For a lender, do not become the advocate. Do not add comfort because the timetable is real. A missing second path is a missing second path on the day money moves, not a detail to be solved in operations later. Later is a condition, chapter 31, or it is a limit in the opinion. It is not a soft yes.

For a buyer, do not become the operator. They will ask what you would fix first, what you would budget, what you would renegotiate. The first of those can be a numbered finding already in the opinion. The budget and the negotiation are not your visit. A list of projects you would run is a design. You were not engaged to design. If they want that, it is a different paper, after they own it, with a different sheet.

Do not keep two opinions in one report, one cautious for the lender and one warmer for the sponsor who paid the invoice. One look, one set of findings, one sentence. The reader is named in the engagement. If both will read it, the sentence is the narrower one, and you say both are readers. A warmer annex for the sponsor is a second opinion. Second opinions in the same week are how the careful one gets discarded.

Your duty runs to the sentence you signed, not to the party who was kindest on site. Kindness is not evidence. Access that depended on kindness is a limit if it can be withdrawn. Write the access you actually had. Chapter 26. A visit that saw only what a host selected is a selected visit. The opinion says selected, or it pretends to have seen the plant.

If the sponsor paid the invoice and the lender is the reader, the sponsor does not get a private softening. Payment is not a second engagement. Say that when the invoice is sent, not when the draft comes back with two sets of comments. Two sets of comments are answered in one sheet. The narrower sentence wins. A finding deleted for the sponsor and kept for the lender is two opinions. You do not keep two.

## Tailoring Technical Risk Weighting: Project Finance Debt Lenders vs. Equity Acquirers

In institutional M&A acquisitions and project finance underwriting for mission-critical data centers, every qualitative observation recorded during the site walk must be translated into a deterministic commercial or contractual parameter for the investment committee and lending syndicate:

$$\text{Lender Focus} = \text{DSCR Downside Survival}; \quad \text{Buyer Focus} = \text{Expansion \& Lifecycle CapEx}$$
