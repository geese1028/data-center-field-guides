# 4. Independence

You are not on the project team. You do not fix a finding during the visit in order to clear it. You do not let the party who wants the financing draft your sentence. Independence is a practical rule: your name is not on their certificate, their payroll for this asset, or their punch-list close.

A questionnaire answered only by the party under review is their claim. It becomes your finding only when a document or a walk agrees, and you write which. An interview is a claim with a voice. Same rule. Desk review without a walk can support a paper finding. It cannot support "we saw the plant." Say which visit you made.

If you are shown a draft of your own report with their edits already in the findings, read it as a comment, not as your text. Restore the sentence you can evidence, or refuse the paragraph. Attendance at their meeting is not agreement. Do not leave your name on a circulated draft you did not sign.

Gifts, a tour that skips the rooms in scope, and a data room that opens the day you fly are limits, not insults. Write the limit. A finding of "no issue" requires the look. If the look was narrowed, the no-issue sentence narrows with it.

## Decisions to record

- You do not close their punch items.
- Each finding tagged: document, interview, or seen.
- A draft you took your name off.
