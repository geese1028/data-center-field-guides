# 5. Papers before the plane

The visit is short. The reading is not. Ask for the papers before you travel, with enough days to actually read them. A data room that opens on the morning of the walk is a tour. You may still walk. You may not sign a review of documents you have not read.

The minimum set, if the engagement is a lender's technical look at a delivered hall:

The engagement, and the certifications it names. The contract that states the performance, cited, not pasted. The split of chairs: who provides the room, who operates, who the customer is. Permits the counsel will rely on, listed, so you know which ones you are not opining on. The level map or test pack, if they claim commissioning. The as-operated drawings only far enough to know design from as-built. A duty roster. A spare rule. The procedures a night shift can find: standing, change, and emergency. Not the whole binder. The index, and the three you will sample.

You do not demand a market study, a serial dump, or a point list. A serial dump is their asset file. Your sheet will say whether identity was checked, and the rule, not the numbers. A point list is not a quality mark. The monitor book already refused that substitution.

If a paper is missing, write missing before you fly, so the site visit is not spent hunting it. A paper produced on site is read later, or it is marked unread. Unread is not accepted.

## What you do with a late room

If the room opens after you have traveled, you do not pretend the week of reading happened on site. You list what you could read in the hours you had, and you list the rest as not read. A speed-read of a contract on a phone is not a review of the contract. Say skimmed, or say not read. Skimmed does not support a sentence about a clause you might have missed. The clauses that match the performance sentence are either opened, or the performance review waits.

Do not let the host walk you through the documents instead of giving them to you. A walkthrough is an interview about the documents. It is not the documents. Ask for the file. If the file cannot leave a screen they control, read it there and write the revision and the time. A screen they scroll for you, too fast to read, is not read.

## Decisions to record

- Papers requested, and the date the room opened.
- Missing papers, written before the walk.
- Drawings told apart: design or as-built, or not accepted as current.
