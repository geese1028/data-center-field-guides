# 26. The week before you fly

The visit is lost in the week before, more often than on the floor. You do not fly to discover the question. The engagement already has the question, or you do not have a job yet.

Read the engagement until you can say, in one sentence, what they will quote you for. A lender's technical opinion. A buyer's condition. A list of certifications copied from a facility agreement. If that sentence is wider than the access you have been promised, write the gap now. Access promised in a slide is not access. Access is a named room, a named data room, and a named person who can open both. If any of the three is "to be confirmed," the opinion that needs them is not yet available. Say so before the ticket is bought. Buying the ticket is not acceptance of a vague scope.

Conflicts are part of the week, not a footer. If you have worked for one of the chairs on this asset, write it to the reader before they rely on you. Independence is not a feeling. It is the absence of a role on their certificate, their payroll for this plant, and their punch close. A previous visit for a different reader, on a different question, is not automatically a conflict. It is also not a head start you may hide. The sample from that visit does not transfer. Chapter 17. Tell them you have seen the hall before, and that this sheet starts empty.

Ask for the papers in chapter 5 with dates. A room that will open "when you land" is a room you will not have read. Put the missing list in writing. Copy their performance sentence onto the sheet before you are persuaded by the architecture slides. You need their words unedited.

Do not build the opinion on the plane from a previous report of yours. A previous report is another hall, even when you wrote it. The headings travel. The findings do not.

## Decisions to record

- The sentence they will quote, copied before travel.
- Access named: room, data room, person. Or not confirmed, and the opinion not yet available.
- Conflicts written to the reader, including a prior visit.
- Papers still missing, listed before the flight.
