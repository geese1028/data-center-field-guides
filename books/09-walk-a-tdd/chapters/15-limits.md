# 15. What this book does not own

It does not own a site. No address, no party name, no serial list, no single-line diagram, no photograph of their hall. The method is the sheet. Their report stays in their file.

It does not own a failure-rate formula, a market forecast, or a legal opinion. Counsel owns permits and remedies. You own whether the technical assumption is evidenced.

It does not own the commissioning sequence, the file judgment, the monitor rules, or the receiver's refusal. It points at them. A green tile is still not acceptance. A heat curve is still not a pass. A signature is still a sentence.

It does not own a sample percentage for the industry. Each visit writes its own rule and follows it.

It does not own the tenant's system. Crossing into applications because they were on a screen is how a facilities walk becomes a false audit.

It does not own the invoice, the timetable, or the reader's anxiety. Those are real. They do not widen a sentence. A late night is not a reason to sign spoken redundancy as tested. Spoken remains spoken.
