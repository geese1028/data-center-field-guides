# 30. One visit, as a method

This chapter walks one visit so the sheet has a shape. Nothing in it is a site. There is no address, no party, no serial, and no load. Where a number would have been, the text says "the performance sentence" or "the sample you wrote."

The reader is a lender. The engagement asks you to say whether the operator can deliver the equipment that was bought, in a room someone else runs, to a customer who specified it. It does not ask you to audit the customer's jobs. You write that line in the first paragraph you will ever send, before you describe a room.

## Before

The data room opens late. You list the unread documents before you fly. You do not treat the flight as a review of them. On the plane you have their performance sentence, copied, and a sample rule: identity on a written fraction of rows, running state on a written few machines, not a feeling. You do not bring a failure-rate formula. You do not bring last year's findings.

## There

Three chairs. The room provider owes power and cooling. The operator owes the equipment, the spares, and the night ticket. The customer owes the specification and their own acceptance test. A service level in the customer contract assumes all three. You do not let the operator's confidence clear the room provider's untested second feed.

You count against their list. The totals meet. You do not publish the list. The identity sample matches, except one function the record calls present and you do not find. That is a finding, five lines, not a story about a busy dock. The running sample includes a dark machine. You write dark. You do not average it into the row.

Their platform test is a document from the week before, signed, scope narrower than the contract title. You accept it as a document for that scope. You do not witness it. You do not let the title widen your verb. A demonstration they offer in the afternoon, outside the script, is refused as a test.

The person who would be called cannot say the first safe state. The binder has an emergency paper of many pages. You find that the paper is not issued. You do not grade the person. The spare list is long. The part the job will not start without is not on the shelf. List only, not checked. That is the sentence.

Power is a drawing you accept as current enough to see two paths drawn, and a test record for one of them. The second is spoken. Spoken does not enter the opinion as tested. Design kilowatts stay out of the cell.

## The opinion

You sign that the count reconciled, that the identity sample matched except the numbered finding, that one test document exists for a narrower scope, and that you did not audit the customer's system. You do not sign that the performance level is achieved. The chair map is incomplete where the second power path is only a speech, and the spare is only a list. Those limits sit in the same section as the yes. A market paragraph is not in the draft. A probability is not in the draft.

They ask you to delete the dark machine because it was "about to be fixed." You do not. They send a document the next week. You read it. You close that number or you do not. You do not reissue a broad no-issue.

They also ask whether the model load is fair. The model uses a design figure. You tag it as design and do not replace it with a number of your own. Insurance adequacy is not asked in a way you can answer. You do not answer it. Chapter 32. The door sentence and the opinion stay the same width. You do not add "overall satisfactory" in the covering mail. The covering mail is part of the opinion if they will forward it. Write it as narrowly as the sheet.

## What this visit was for

You leave with their sentence copied, your sentence narrower, findings numbered, rooms you did not enter listed, and no site a later reader can name. That is a due diligence. A thicker report that hides the speech and the dark machine is not.

## Decisions to record

- Line written before any room description.
- Four acts: count, identity, running state, their document. Each tagged.
- Performance sentence not signed, and the two reasons.
- A requested deletion refused.
