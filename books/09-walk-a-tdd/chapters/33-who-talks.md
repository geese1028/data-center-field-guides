# 33. Talk to the person who would be called

An interview with the person who arranged your visit will be fluent. It is the wrong interview if that person will not be called when the plant leaves the state the contract assumes. Ask for the name on the roster for the hour that matters. If they are off shift, wait, or write that you did not hear the stop from the person who owes it. A deputy they appoint in the corridor is a deputy only if the roster already names them. A deputy invented for your visit is an interview with a guide.

Ask them to say the first safe state without the binder. Then open the binder and see if the paper matches what they said. A match is evidence the paper is issued. A mismatch is a contradiction, chapter 24. Do not coach them to the paper's words and then record a match. Coaching is your voice. Your voice is not their issuance.

Ask what they did the last time something in scope failed, if they will say it without names and without a performance. You are listening for whether a record exists, not for a story you can repeat. If the record cannot be opened, the story is an interview. If they will not speak without a manager in the room, write that. A managed interview is still an interview. It is weaker. Do not upgrade it because the manager nodded.

You do not interview the tenant's users about their jobs, unless that layer is in scope. A friendly explanation of the workload will attach itself to your opinion if you write it down as fact. Write "not in scope" and stop. The same for the room provider's designer who wants to walk you through intent. Intent is not the as-built. Thank them and ask for the revision you already required.

Two interviews that disagree are not averaged. Tag both. The person who operates tonight outweighs the person who sold the financing, on the question of what they can do tonight. The contract outweighs both, on the question of what is owed.

## Decisions to record

- Name on the roster, spoken to, or not available and written as not heard.
- Stop said before the binder was opened. Match or mismatch.
- A managed interview tagged as managed.
- Tenant workload and designer intent not written as findings.
