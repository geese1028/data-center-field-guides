# 28. Hardware identity is not an application audit

The financing often depends on the machines being the machines that were bought. That is an identity question. It is not a question about what the tenant runs on them.

Identity has three checks, and they are not interchangeable. The thing is present. The record matches what you see, on the sample, by the rule you wrote. The configuration the contract assumed is the configuration in front of you, again on the sample. A full row of lights does not answer any of the three. A front panel that says the family name does not answer the configuration. Open the record, or write that you did not.

You will be offered a dashboard of jobs, a training screen, a utilization chart. That is above the line in chapter 2 unless the engagement put it in scope and gave you a way to evidence it. A chart is not a count of cards. A conversation about models is not a serial check. Thank them and return to the asset record. Crossing the line to look busy will be quoted as an audit of the tenant.

Licenses sit beside identity, not inside it. The machine can match and the right to run the feature they sell can be absent. Ask for the document that names this use. An interview that says the license is complete is an interview. Do not fold it into "equipment verified."

A mismatch is a finding even if the substitute is newer or "better." Better is not what was bought, unless the contract allows the substitute and a document says this unit is that substitute. Write the function that did not match. Do not write their serials into the opinion. The asset file keeps the identifiers. Your sentence says the sample matched, did not match, or was not finished.

## Decisions to record

- Present, record match, and configuration, as three results.
- A dashboard you did not treat as a count.
- License kept as a separate question.
- A "better" substitute not accepted unless the contract allows it and a document names it.
