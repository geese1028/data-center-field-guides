# 25. After you send it

The opinion leaves. The hall keeps running. Your job is not to become their operator, and not to become their advocate when the lender asks a wider question than you signed.

If a question arrives that your sheet already refused, answer with the sheet. Do not add a sentence on a call that the written opinion does not contain. Calls get remembered as certifications. If the question needs a new look, it is a new visit, with a new sheet, or it is not answered. Do not extend the first visit by telephone.

If they close a finding, read the close as a new document. Write whether that numbered finding is closed, and leave the others open. Do not reissue a broad no-issue because one number died. A stack of closes is not a new opinion unless you write one, on purpose, after the last gate you cared about is actually met.

Do not present the visit. No slides of their drawings, no serials, no photograph of the room in a talk, no market adjectives you refused in the opinion. The method you may reuse is the sheet. The report is theirs and the lender's. Your name on a conference story is a second publication you did not clear.

Keep your notes as long as the engagement says, then stop holding their asset list in a place a later project can stumble on. A later visit does not inherit this sample. Chapter 17. A notebook full of identifiers is not a template. The template is the headings, empty.

## Decisions to record

- A wider question answered only from the sheet, or refused pending a new visit.
- Findings closed one number at a time.
- No second publication of their hall.
- Identifiers not carried into the next engagement.
