# 36. An O&M contract is not a roster

They will hand you an operation agreement and a slide of a team. The agreement is what is owed between chairs. The roster is who is there. The procedure is what that person can do. Three documents. A due diligence that reviews only the agreement has reviewed a promise.

Read the agreement for the obligations that match the performance sentence. Response, presence, maintenance, spares, reporting. Cite the clause by heading, not by pasting it. Then ask whether the roster covers the hours those clauses assume. A contract that requires a person on site, and a roster that shows a call-out from another city, are a contradiction. Do not decide that call-out is "equivalent." Equivalent is a smoothing. Write both.

Then the sample of procedures, chapter 9. The agreement can require an emergency paper the panel does not have. The panel can have a paper the agreement does not mention. The person can describe a third version. Three versions are a finding that the paper is not issued. You do not pick the best written one and call the system mature.

Reporting clauses are easy to overlook and easy to fake. The agreement says monthly reports. Ask for one month, by revision, and whether the open items in it match the questionnaire. A report that says none, while you have already seen a dark machine, is a false document if the machine is in its scope. If the machine is the operator's and the report is the room provider's, do not charge one chair with the other's silence. Separate the chairs first.

Price, penalty, and term are counsel's, unless a technical assumption sits inside them. You do not say the fee is enough to fund the roster. Enough is a model. You say whether the roster you saw can do the presence the clause names. If you did not see the roster, you did not review the operation. You reviewed a contract.

## Decisions to record

- Agreement clauses that match the performance sentence, cited by heading.
- Roster hours against those clauses. Equivalence refused.
- Procedure versions: agreement, panel, person. Mismatch is not issued.
- One monthly report checked against known open items, or not reviewed.
