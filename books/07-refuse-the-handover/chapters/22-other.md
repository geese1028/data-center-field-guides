# 22. Another hall is not this pack

They will name a previous project. It signed. It is running. Therefore this page should be signed. That is an analogy, not evidence. You were not asked to accept the other hall in this meeting, and you may not use its signature as a sheet that is missing here. The other hall's file, model, screen, and punch are not in this pack. Even if you worked that hall, your memory is not an appendix. Chapter 2.

Do not confirm or deny their story about the other project. Confirming it imports a site into this record. Denying it starts a second argument that still does not fill this pack. Say that this signature uses this pack. Then return to the gate.

## What does not transfer

A factory certificate from a sister machine does not transfer. Chapter 10. A setpoint that was acceptable there does not transfer, unless it is written in this specification and this file judgment used it. A screen layout that looked green there does not accept this image. A waiver they lived with there is not a waiver here, unless you can operate this defect and you write the sentence again, for this plant, with an end date. Copying a waiver across halls is how a local defect becomes a standard. You do not found a standard in a handover meeting.

A code they say was accepted there, without the edition and without the sheet, does not transfer either. You still do not open the code in this meeting. You also do not treat their recollection of the code as the sheet. Titles, if the contract names them, are cited in their file. Recollection is not a citation.

## When you did work the other hall

If you know the other project failed a gate you are being asked to wave through, you still do not tell that story in this record. You refuse this pack on this pack's gaps. Their gossip is not your evidence, and your gossip is not a source. The useful fact, if any, is a method you already wrote into a sheet on that job, with the names removed. You may apply the method. You may not attach the old sheet. Attaching it identifies the hall. Chapter 16.

If the other hall is their proof that "everyone signs," everyone is not a witness. Chapter 11. A room full of previous signatures is a chorus, chapter 13, not a control. You are one name. The name signs this sentence or it does not.

## Decisions to record

- The other project named, and that you did not use it as evidence. You do not need to record the name. "Another project was cited. Not used."
- A certificate, setpoint, waiver, or screen you refused to copy across.
- A story about a previous failure you did not put in this record.
