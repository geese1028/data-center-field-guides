# 11. A letter is not a witness

For each script they want you to treat as proved, the pack says whether someone witnessed it or accepted a document, and where that document is. The third book put that distinction in the short pack. You use it as a gate. If you cannot tell the two apart, you do not sign the proved-sentence for that script. A document can be honest and still not be a witness. A witness can be absent and the document can still be filed. The signature has to say which one you are relying on.

Sampling belongs in the same place. If they did not witness every line, the pack says what was sampled and what rule picked the sample. "We saw enough" is not a rule. You do not invent a sampling rate in the meeting, and you do not copy one from a code you are not opening. If the claim was that every system was witnessed and the pack shows a sample, the claim is wider than the evidence. Strike the claim. A sample can still support a sentence about the sample. It cannot support a sentence about the hall.

## Documents that do not close a fail

A letter that says a setting was adjusted is not a witnessed changeover. A photograph of a label is not a leak test. A vendor's remote note that a value looked normal is not your file judgment. The second book’s sentence is not replaced by their adjective. If the close of a punch line rests on one of these, chapter 8 already told you not to inherit the close. This chapter tells you not to let the same paper prove the script.

A document you cannot open is not a document. A path that only the author can reach fails chapter 3. Ask a second person to open it before you rely on it. If they cannot, you are relying on a name, not on a record.

## What you may accept

You may accept a witnessed script and refuse a documented one, or the reverse, if you write which. You may accept a sample for the systems in the sample. You may file the unwitnessed rest as not proved. None of that requires you to insult the document. It requires you to keep the verb accurate. Witnessed, accepted as a document, or not done. The third book’s three words are enough. Do not add "substantially complete." Substantially is a fourth word that means not done, wearing a better coat.

## Decisions to record

- For each proved-sentence: witnessed, accepted as a document, or not done.
- Sample rule written, or the hall-wide claim struck.
- A letter or a photograph you did not allow to close a fail.
- A path a second person could not open, not relied on.
