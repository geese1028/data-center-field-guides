# 13. Who may sign

The person who signs the operator's sentence is the person who will be asked why the hall was accepted, or their named deputy for this meeting, written before the meeting. Not the person who wants the meeting to end. Not the vendor. Not the tester countersigning their own pack. Not a delegate invented in the room because the named person is on a call.

If you are not that person, you may attend, you may refuse to advise a yes, and you may not sign. Advising is not signing. Signing because you were the only one still in the room is how the wrong name gets on the page. Leave the page unsigned. An unsigned page is a delay. A wrong name is a false document.

## Read it, then sign it

You sign only a page you have read, including the attachments it incorporates. If the pack is too long to read in the meeting, the meeting is not the signature. You take the pack, you read the sheets in chapter 3, and you sign in a later meeting or you refuse. Speed is not a duty. The duty is the match between sentence and evidence.

A page in a language you cannot read is a page you have not read, unless a named person translates it into the record and signs the translation. You do not sign on the strength of a summary spoken across the table. Spoken summaries are the kinder sentences chapter 4 already refused.

If your company requires a second signature, the second person has the same duty. They do not sign because you did. They read the sentence and the limits. Two signatures on an unread page are not a control. They are a chorus.

## What you say in the room

You say your sentence, and you say what you are not signing. You do not argue the plant's reputation, the schedule, or the cost of delay. Those facts may be real. They do not change a missing sheet into a present one. If someone needs the schedule recorded, they can record it on a different page. It does not go under your name as a reason you accepted evidence you did not have.

You do not promise to "sort it out after signature." After signature is when the wide sentence starts being quoted. Sort it out before, or write the condition in the same document, with an end date. Chapter 12.

## Decisions to record

- The name allowed to sign the operator's sentence, written before the meeting.
- Pages unread, therefore unsigned.
- A spoken summary you did not treat as a translation.
- Schedule and cost kept off your signature page.
