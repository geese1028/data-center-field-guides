# 18. A renamed drawing is not as-built

They will offer drawings as proof the hall is what was tested. A drawing supports a signature only if you can say what it is a drawing of. Design, shop issue, and as-built are three documents. A design file with a new cover that says as-built is still the design. You refuse any sentence that treats it as the plant you will operate.

You do not check the drawing against a code in the meeting, and you do not redline it into compliance. That is a different job. Your check is whether the pack can tell the three documents apart, and whether the operator pages match the one they claim is current.

## What you refuse

![As-Built vs. Redline Forensic Custody Audit](../figures/fig-03-as-built-redline-custody-audit.png)


A single PDF that contains every revision, with no mark of which revision was present on the day of the test. The test proves the plant that was there, not the plant in the latest cloud copy. If they cannot point to the revision witnessed, the drawing does not support the proved-sentence. The file judgment and the witness note have to name a revision, or they are floating.

A drawing that still shows a machine, a valve, or a setpoint the punch print says was moved. Either the drawing is late or the punch is late. You do not choose the prettier one. Both stay open until they agree, and you do not sign that the plant matches the drawing. Matching is a sentence. Disagreement is the evidence.

A point on a graphic that is not the point on the operator page. The sixth book already refused a screen that disagrees with a walked reading. A graphic that disagrees with the stop page is the same defect in ink. The page wins until the graphic is corrected. You do not accept the graphic as the handover of the screen.

A drawing used as a point list. Identifiers on a sheet are their file. You do not paste them into your sheet to look thorough. Your question remains the one in chapter 6: which points may speak. A dense drawing is not an answer.

## What you may accept

You may accept a design drawing as design, filed, not used as the hall. Write that limit. It is the same shape as accepting a model as a picture. Useful to the next change. Not a measurement of what was built.

You may accept an as-built only when a named person says it was checked against the plant after the last change that matters to your sentence, and the date of that check is on the sheet. "Checked" means they can say what they looked at. A signature block with no date is not a check. If the last change is still an open punch line, the as-built is not current. Chapter 8. Do not let a CAD date substitute for the close of the line.

Partial floors and partial suites follow the scope letter. A drawing of the whole building does not prove the suite you are being asked to run, and a drawing of the suite does not prove the building. Strike the extra words. Chapter 1.

## Field-Walked As-Built Redline Verification vs. Renamed Design CAD

### Statistical Sampling of Physical Breaker, Valve, and Sensor Tags

In institutional data center project delivery, the transition from construction and commissioning (ASHRAE Guideline 0 / Guideline 202 Levels 1 through 5) to live 24/7 operations is governed by measurable engineering gates rather than schedule milestones. Whenever a handover package asks the receiving operations lead to certify a plant attribute, that attribute must satisfy the quantitative gating relationship:

$$\varepsilon_{\text{redline}} = \frac{N_{\text{mismatched\_tags}}}{N_{\text{walked\_sample}}} = 0\% \text{ across } n = 25 \text{ nodes}$$

If any supporting evidence file, witnessed test script, or as-built redline verification falls outside its acceptance corridor, the operations representative must either issue a formal **Notice of Handover Refusal** or execute a narrow **Conditional Acceptance with 200% Retainage Escrow** that explicitly bounds the permitted operating envelope until the defect is cured and re-tested.

## Decisions to record

- Design, shop, and as-built told apart, or the drawing sentence refused.
- Revision tied to the witnessed test, or the drawing not used for the proved-sentence.
- Graphic and operator page: page wins where they disagree.
- As-built check dated after the last change that your sentence depends on, or not accepted as current.
