# Tech translate

Do not start while the English edition is unaccepted, or before 30 days on sale, or for a book the owner has not named as selling.

Translate the accepted English, not the Chinese source files. Keep citations, gate names, and the rule that numbers come from the specification then in force. Do not add local client names. Declare the translation as AI-assisted when the upload pack is made.

Order, one language at a time: German, Japanese, Spanish, French, Portuguese. Stop after the language the owner named.
