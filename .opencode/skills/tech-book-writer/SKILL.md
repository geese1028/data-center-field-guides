# Tech book writer

Write a new English field guide. Do not translate the source files. Do not paste them.

## Rules

The reader finishes able to fill a sheet and fail a gate. Each chapter ends with decisions to record. Unconfirmed interface numbers stay as “cite the specification then in force.” Client names, site identifiers, drawings, addresses, and quotation figures stay out.

Public pages may be cited by title, URL, and the date read. Short published configuration facts may be stated with that citation. Marketing multiples are not duty. Memory bandwidth is not coolant flow.

Vendor specifications in the working file are patterns: named metal, capped clean pipe, isolation, a factory water-and-power run, a command path from script to load switch. Do not copy their dimensions into the book.

## Length

Follow the series brief. Do not pad. Do not start cover, EPUB, or translation.
