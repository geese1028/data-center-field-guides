# Publish and promote

Prepare the upload pack. The owner uploads it.

The pack is a folder: EPUB path, cover path, title, subtitle, description under 4000 characters, seven keywords, two browse categories, price band from the last Amazon check, and the AI-content disclosure. Amazon and other stores together. Not KDP Select.

Do not log into KDP. Do not buy reviews. Do not place ads. Do not invent a rank or a review count.
