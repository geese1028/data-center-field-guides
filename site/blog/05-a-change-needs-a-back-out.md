# A data center change needs a back-out

![Old state, new state, and a way back.](figures/mop.png)

*Original diagram. Not a site record.*


Work that changes the plant is not a task on a list. It is a change with an old state, a new state, a person who accepted it, and a way back. If the way back is "call the vendor," write that. It is a limit. It is more honest than a blank.

The shift has to be able to see the abnormal state before the window starts. A change that lives only in a chat will be paged as a fault, and then argued as if nobody knew. Mark it where the people and the screen can both see it, or do not open the window.

A back-out that has never been read aloud is not a back-out. The person on the night has to be able to say the stop without opening a slide deck. If they cannot, the job waits. Speed is not a reason to skip the sentence.

Do not paste a customer's method statement into a public note. Their hazards stay in their file. Your record names the stop, the back-out, and whether the service was confirmed after, not merely that the alarm went quiet.
